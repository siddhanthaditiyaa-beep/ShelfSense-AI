ShelfSense AI started as an internship project and evolved into a full-scale multi-agent retail intelligence platform with 40 AI agents, 13 security layers, and 332 features.

📖 Our Story

ShelfSense AI was born from a simple observation — retail store owners spend hours manually checking stock, missing theft incidents, and losing revenue to poor pricing decisions. As Computer Engineering students, we saw not just an inventory problem, but a security problem.

Siddhanthaditiyaa Vettakal and Sneha Pillai, Computer Engineering students at Pillai College of Engineering, Mumbai, combined their expertise in cybersecurity, machine learning, and full-stack development to build a platform that doesn't just manage inventory — it actively protects it.

What started as an academic internship project grew into a comprehensive multi-agent AI system featuring YOLOv8 computer vision, real-time anomaly detection, dynamic pricing, a multi-store marketplace, and 13-layer cybersecurity — all working together to give retail store owners their time back.

🚀 What We Built

ShelfSense AI is a complete SaaS platform — not just a dashboard. Here's what's inside:

🤖
40 AI Agents
18 dashboard-controlled with kill switch, 22 background agents running silently. Covers monitoring, forecasting, fraud, pricing, weather, supplier, and more.
📸
YOLOv8 Shelf Detection
Custom-trained model on Roboflow retail dataset. Upload shelf images via Google Colab + ngrok tunnel — detects occupied and empty slots automatically.
🏪
Multi-Store Marketplace
Customers can discover multiple stores, browse live inventory, and shop from any store. Store owners see their own customer analytics.
🛡️
13-Layer Cybersecurity
ISO 27001 (93%), NIST (88%), OWASP (100%), PCI-DSS (85%) compliance. JWT blacklisting, rate limiting, honeypots, biometric anomaly detection.
💳
Integrated Payments
Razorpay UPI/card/netbanking gateway, Google OAuth login, real-time order tracking, customer loyalty points and referral system.
📊
Deep Analytics
40+ analytics pages — funnel analytics, customer LTV, cohort revenue, demand heatmaps, GST reports, P&L statements, and AI-powered forecasting.
🤖
Groq LLaMA3 Chatbot
Natural language queries about your store — "Which products run out this week?" gets an instant AI answer from live store data.
📍
Nearby Franchise Locator
When a product is out of stock, instantly find the nearest franchise store with available inventory and display distance in km.
🔔
Real-Time Alerts
Email alerts, Telegram notifications, UptimeRobot monitoring, server self-ping every 4 minutes, voice alert reading.

🛠️ How We Built It

ShelfSense AI was built across 12 development batches, combining multiple cutting-edge technologies into a single cohesive platform.

1

Started with the problem

Visited local retail stores in Mumbai to understand real pain points — manual stock checking, missed theft, inefficient ordering, no data visibility.

2

Designed the multi-agent architecture

Architected a system of 40 specialized AI agents using Node.js and node-cron, each with a specific role — monitoring, forecasting, fraud detection, pricing, and more.

3

Trained YOLOv8 on retail shelf data

Fine-tuned a custom YOLOv8 model using Google Colab free GPU on a Roboflow retail shelf dataset. Integrated via ngrok tunnel for live shelf scanning from the dashboard.

4

Built 13-layer cybersecurity

Implemented OWASP Top 10 protections, JWT blacklisting, bcrypt, rate limiting, honeypots, biometric anomaly detection, and full audit logging — achieving ISO 27001 93% compliance.

5

Built the multi-store marketplace

Added a public store discovery page where customers can browse all stores, compare inventory, and shop across multiple store owners — all from one platform.

6

Integrated AI chatbot + deep analytics

Wired Groq LLaMA3 for natural language store queries. Built 40+ analytics pages covering every aspect of retail operations — targeting IEEE paper publication.

7

Deployed on Render with full PWA support

Live at shelfsense-ai-lptz.onrender.com with UptimeRobot monitoring, server self-ping keep-alive, service worker PWA, and 10 UI themes.
