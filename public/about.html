📖 Our Story

ShelfSense AI was born from a simple observation — retail store owners spend hours manually checking stock, missing theft incidents, and losing revenue to poor pricing decisions. As Computer Engineering Students, we saw not just an inventory problem, but a security problem.

Siddhanthaditiyaa and Sneha, Computer Engineering Students at Pillai College of Engineering, Mumbai, combined their expertise in security systems, machine learning, and full-stack development to build a platform that doesn't just manage inventory — it actively protects it.

What started as an academic internship project became a comprehensive multi-agent AI system featuring YOLOv8 computer vision, real-time anomaly detection, dynamic pricing algorithms, and a secure payment gateway — all working together to give retail store owners their time back.

🛠️ How We Built It

ShelfSense AI was built over weeks of intensive development, combining multiple cutting-edge technologies into a single cohesive platform.

1

Started with the problem

Visited local retail stores in Mumbai to understand pain points — manual stock checking, missed theft, inefficient ordering.

2

Trained YOLOv8 on retail shelf data

Collected and trained a custom YOLOv8 model on retail shelf images from Roboflow dataset to detect occupied and empty slots with high accuracy.

3

Built the multi-agent system

Designed and implemented 10 specialized AI agents using Node.js and node-cron, each with a specific role in the retail management pipeline.

4

Added the cybersecurity layer

Implemented 9 security layers including Helmet.js, rate limiting, JWT authentication, bcrypt hashing, MongoDB injection prevention, and time-aware anomaly detection.

5

Launched ShelfSense AI

Deployed the complete platform with a beautiful customer-facing shop, admin dashboard, Razorpay payments, and real-time email alerts.
