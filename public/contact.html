FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about ShelfSense AI.

Is ShelfSense AI free to use? ▼
Yes! We offer a free plan with up to 10 products and basic monitoring. You can upgrade to Pro (₹999/month) or Enterprise (₹2,999/month) for full access to all 10 AI agents.
How does the theft detection work? ▼
Our Anomaly Detection Agent uses Z-score statistical analysis to monitor stock levels. If an unusual stock drop is detected outside your configured shop hours, it immediately sends an urgent email alert to your registered address.
Do I need technical knowledge to use ShelfSense AI? ▼
Not at all! ShelfSense AI is designed to be used by anyone. Simply sign up, add your products, set your shop hours, and all 10 AI agents start working automatically.
How secure is my store's data? ▼
We implement 9 layers of cybersecurity including JWT authentication, bcrypt password hashing, rate limiting, brute force protection, MongoDB injection prevention, and CORS protection. Your data is always encrypted and isolated.
Can I use ShelfSense AI for multiple store locations? ▼
Multiple store support is available in our Enterprise plan (₹2,999/month). This includes a super admin dashboard to manage all locations from one place.
What is the YOLOv8 shelf scanning feature? ▼
You can upload a photo of your store shelf, and our custom-trained YOLOv8 AI model will automatically detect which slots are occupied and which are empty, helping you identify out-of-stock positions instantly.
