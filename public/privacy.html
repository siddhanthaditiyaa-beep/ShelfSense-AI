🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026 · Effective immediately

🛡️ ShelfSense AI is built by Computer Engineering Students. We take your privacy extremely seriously. This policy explains exactly what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

1. 📋 Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information when you use ShelfSense AI:

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, and encrypted password when you create an account.
  • Store Information: Store name, location, opening/closing hours, and inventory data you add to the platform.
  • Order Data: Purchase history, cart items, and payment transaction IDs (we never store full card details).
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, and interactions with the dashboard for improving our service.
  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, and device type for security monitoring purposes.

2. 🎯 How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Providing and improving the ShelfSense AI platform and its 10 AI agents
  • Sending critical security alerts and stock notifications to your registered email
  • Processing payments securely through our Razorpay integration
  • Detecting and preventing fraudulent or unauthorized access to your account
  • Analyzing aggregate usage patterns to improve platform performance
  • Responding to your support requests and inquiries

3. 🔐 How We Protect Your Data

As a cybersecurity-focused platform, we implement multiple layers of protection:

  • Password Encryption: All passwords are hashed using bcrypt with a cost factor of 12 — industry-leading strength.
  • JWT Authentication: Secure token-based authentication with 24-hour expiry.
  • Rate Limiting: Brute force attack prevention with automatic account lockout after 5 failed attempts.
  • MongoDB Sanitization: All database queries are sanitized to prevent injection attacks.
  • HTTPS Encryption: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted.
  • Security Headers: Helmet.js security headers protect against clickjacking, XSS, and sniffing attacks.

4. 💳 Payment Information

All payments are processed by Razorpay, a PCI DSS compliant payment gateway. ShelfSense AI never stores, processes, or transmits your full card number, CVV, or banking credentials. We only store the Razorpay payment ID for order reference purposes.

5. 📧 Email Communications

We send emails only for the following reasons:

  • Critical stock alerts (out of stock, low stock) from our Monitoring Agent
  • Security alerts (suspicious activity, theft detection) from our Anomaly Detection Agent
  • Purchase order notifications from our Supplier Agent
  • Product expiry warnings from our Expiry Agent
  • Account-related communications (password reset, account verification)

We do not send marketing emails without your explicit consent.

6. 🤝 Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data only with:

  • Razorpay: For payment processing (subject to their privacy policy)
  • Open-Meteo API: Only your store's city name for weather data (no personal data shared)
  • Legal authorities: Only when required by law or to protect the safety of our users

7. 🍪 Cookies

ShelfSense AI uses minimal cookies and local storage for:

  • Storing your authentication token (JWT) for session management
  • Remembering your theme preference (light/dark/system)
  • Saving your wishlist items locally on your device

We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking cookies.

8. 👤 Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: Request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Correction: Update or correct your personal information at any time
  • Deletion: Request complete deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Portability: Export your store data in a readable format

To exercise any of these rights, email us at shelfsense.alerts@gmail.com

9. 📅 Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all your personal data is permanently removed within 30 days. Order history may be retained for legal compliance purposes for up to 7 years.
